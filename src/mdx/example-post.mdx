# My First Blog Post

Welcome to your new blog! This is an example post to show you how to structure your articles.

## Frontmatter

Every post needs frontmatter at the top:

```yaml
---
title: Your Post Title
description: A brief description
post_date: YYYY-MM-DD
post_category: blog_post  # or non_blog_post for pages
---
```
